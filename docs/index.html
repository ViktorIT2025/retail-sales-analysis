READ BEFORE INTERPRETING

A portfolio analysis, with context.

The bundled dataset is Mohammad Talib's Retail Sales Dataset on Kaggle. It is a synthetic practice dataset; the charts describe these records, not verified real-world business behavior.

  • 1,000 transactions in the original file; 9 source columns.
  • January 1, 2023 through January 1, 2024. January 2024 has only one recorded day and is excluded by the default 2023 filter.
  • Currency is unspecified. Values are shown in dataset currency units.
  • Sales revenue is not profit: the file contains no costs, margins or returns.
  • Each original customer ID appears once. Retention, repeat purchasing and lifetime value cannot be measured.
  • There is no inventory, promotion, location or product-level information. This dashboard cannot establish why a pattern occurred.
  • One year of synthetic data does not establish recurring seasonality or dependable forecasts.

How the measures work

Revenue = sum of Total Amount. Average purchase = revenue ÷ transaction count. Revenue per unit = revenue ÷ units sold, a quantity-weighted price. Shares always use the current filtered selection.

Explaining a change

Revenue = transaction count × average purchase. The comparison splits a change equally across interaction terms: count effect = change in count × mean of the two average purchase values; purchase-value effect = change in average purchase × mean of the two counts. These contributions add exactly to the observed revenue change. Both months need transactions.

Data checks

Files with missing fields, invalid dates, nonpositive quantities or prices, duplicate transaction IDs, or inconsistent totals are rejected. Maximum upload size: 10 MB.
